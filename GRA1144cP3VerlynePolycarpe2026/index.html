01 / THE STORY

EVERYONE

Molly Bloom, a former Olympic-class skier, finds herself running one of the most exclusive underground poker games in the world.

Her games attract celebrities, athletes, business leaders and powerful players. What begins as a small game becomes a multi-million-dollar operation.

When the FBI begins investigating her operation, Molly must fight to protect her name, her business, and the people who trusted her.

Every game has a price.
